# Feasibility Study

## 1. Business model
Who pays, for what, how often. Compare 2–3 model options (subscription, commission,
transaction fee, licensing, ads). Pick one with reasons. Benchmark pricing against
competitors (cite).

## 2. Unit economics
Per customer (or per order):
- Price / ARPU, gross margin (with cost lines), CAC by channel, retention/churn,
  LTV = ARPU × gross margin × lifetime, LTV:CAC, CAC payback months.
- Use sourced benchmarks for the sector and region where possible; label the rest `assumption`.
- Show **bear / base / bull**. Name the 2–3 variables the outcome is most sensitive to.

## 3. Costs & funding
- One-off startup costs (licences, build, legal, inventory, deposits).
- Monthly burn by category (team, tech, marketing, ops, rent).
- Months to break-even per scenario and capital needed to reach it (+ 6 months buffer).
- 3-year simplified P&L per scenario (annual is enough).

## 4. Regulatory & legal
Licences needed, regulator, rough time and cost, data-protection rules (e.g. Saudi
PDPL), sector restrictions, foreign-ownership limits, local-content rules. Cite the regulator.

## 5. Operational & technical
What must work in operations (supply, logistics, partners, support) and in tech
(build vs buy, hard parts, integrations). Rate each 1–5 for difficulty.

## 6. Team
Key roles for the first 12 months and which are hardest to hire locally.

## 7. Risk register
| Risk | Category | Likelihood (1–5) | Impact (1–5) | Mitigation | Early warning signal |

## Output (`04-feasibility.md`)
Shared handoff format, ID prefix `F`. Overall feasibility score 1–5 for:
financial, regulatory, operational, technical, team.
