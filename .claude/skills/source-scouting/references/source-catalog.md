# Source catalogue (starting points — verify each is current)

## Global
- **Macro / population:** World Bank Open Data, IMF WEO, UN Data, OECD.Stat, ITU (digital adoption), GSMA Intelligence (mobile).
- **Industry:** Statista (often paywalled — use the free headline), McKinsey/BCG/Bain/Deloitte/PwC/EY/KPMG insights, Gartner/IDC/Forrester press releases, CB Insights reports.
- **Funding & companies:** Crunchbase, PitchBook (press data), Dealroom, Tracxn, AngelList/Wellfound, company LinkedIn pages.
- **Filings:** SEC EDGAR, Companies House (UK), annual reports of listed peers.
- **Demand signals:** Google Trends, Google Keyword Planner, SimilarWeb, Sensor Tower / data.ai (app ranks), App Store & Google Play reviews, Reddit, Product Hunt, G2/Capterra (B2B), Trustpilot.
- **Patents / tech:** Google Patents, Lens.org, arXiv.

## Saudi Arabia
- **GASTAT** (stats.gov.sa) — population, household spending, labour force, establishments.
- **SAMA** (sama.gov.sa) — payments, fintech, banking stats; **Fintech Saudi** annual reports.
- **Monsha'at** (monshaat.gov.sa) — SME stats and quarterly reports.
- **MISA** (misa.gov.sa) — investment, sector reports, licences.
- **CST** (cst.gov.sa) — telecom & ICT indicators, e-commerce data.
- **Ministry of Commerce** — commercial registrations, e-commerce law.
- **Vision 2030 / VRPs** (vision2030.gov.sa) — sector targets.
- **SDAIA, ZATCA, Tadawul (saudiexchange.sa) filings, Open Data portal (open.data.gov.sa)**.
- **Sector regulators:** SFDA (food/drugs), MoH, MoE, TGA (transport), GEA (entertainment), MoT (tourism), CMA (capital markets), RGA/REGA (real estate).

## GCC / MENA
- **MAGNiTT** — MENA VC funding data; **Wamda**, **Arabnet**, **Wamda/Arab Founders** reports.
- **UAE:** FCSC, Dubai Statistics Center, DET; **Qatar:** PSA; **Kuwait:** CSB; **Egypt:** CAPMAS.
- **Press:** Arab News, Argaam (أرقام), Al Eqtisadiah (الاقتصادية), Asharq Business, Zawya, MEED, Wamda news.
- **Arabic search terms:** حجم سوق، نمو سوق، إحصاءات، تقرير قطاع، عدد المنشآت، إنفاق الأسر، تمويل شركات ناشئة.
